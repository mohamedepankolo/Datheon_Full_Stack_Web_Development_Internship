N’GONI NANA · Project note

Voice interfaces in Bambara

Context

For users with limited formal literacy, a voice-first interface can offer a different way to interact with information and training. N’GONI NANA explores this opportunity in Bambara for rural women in Mali.

Work and contribution

I contribute to the applied AI and interaction work of an ongoing initiative intended to support entrepreneurship education and basic business management. The project is not presented here as a finished commercial product.

What I learned

Designing inclusive voice experiences means paying attention to language, clarity, expectations and real use contexts.

This is a personal project note, based on documented experience. Illustrative imagery is not a screenshot of the project.
