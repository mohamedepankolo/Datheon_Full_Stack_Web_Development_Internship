Leading a national youth organisation

Context

Managing a national volunteer-led organisation requires coordination, transparency and a shared sense of purpose.

Work and contribution

As national president, I worked across three local entities, coordinated more than 150 members and supported projects and international opportunities. This experience shaped how I approach teams and delivery alongside software engineering.

What I learned

Leadership becomes sustainable when workflows, responsibilities and institutional knowledge do not depend on one person.

This is a personal project note, based on documented experience. Illustrative imagery is not a screenshot of the project.
