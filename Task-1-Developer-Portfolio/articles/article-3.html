Finding bias in procurement documents

Context

Public procurement specifications can contain requirements whose wording may unintentionally restrict competition or introduce bias. Analysing such documents systematically is a promising use case for applied NLP.

Work and contribution

During my CITADEL research and development assignment, I contributed to an AI-assisted approach that explored linguistic and structural signals in terms of reference and procurement materials using few-shot methods.

What I learned

The goal was to support human review, not to replace policy or legal decisions with an algorithm.
